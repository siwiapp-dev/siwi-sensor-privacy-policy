Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the SiWi Sensor app (hereby referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by SiWi (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide our BLE sensor monitoring and server authorization services, the Application may collect and process certain information.

  • Precise Location Data: The Application requires access to your device's precise location. This is strictly required by the Android OS to scan for and connect to Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) sensors. We use this permission to discover nearby SiWi hardware. We do not use this data to track your physical movements, nor do we sell it to third parties.
  • Device and Network Information: The Application may collect your device's IP address, operating system version, and the time and duration of your app usage to facilitate secure server authorization and ensure the app functions correctly.
  • Bluetooth Data: The App requires Bluetooth permissions to communicate with your SiWi sensors. Data retrieved from the sensors remains between your device and your local hardware, unless explicitly synced to our secure servers for authorization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Discover, connect to, and configure nearby SiWi BLE sensors.
  • Authenticate your device and update authorizations on our servers.
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the Application.

3. Third-Party Access

We do not share your data with third-party advertising or analytics companies. Data may only be shared if required by law or to comply with legal processes.

4. Data Retention

We retain server authorization logs and usage data for as long as necessary to provide the service. Device-specific data (like IP addresses and usage times) is retained for diagnostic purposes and server security.

5. Account and Data Deletion

If you wish to request the deletion of your account and any associated server data, please contact us at the email provided below. We will process your request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information. We use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This Application is intended for professional, technical, and industrial use. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, or if you require support regarding the Application, do not hesitate to contact us.
